The Marks Weighed

The beast from the sea, one mark at a time: which readings claim each mark, and how their arguments stand.

Home › The Marks Weighed

Draft · awaiting Fr. John's review

The marks are those Scripture gives of the beast from the sea in Revelation 13, set out in depth on The First Beast. Each page here takes one mark and weighs the readings side by side. "Claims" says only whether a reading uses the mark; how well it does is on each page.

# Mark Texts Irenaeus Thiel Fr. John Page

2 Built from Daniel's beasts Revelation 13:1–2; Daniel 7 Yes Partly Yes · probable Weighed
3 A counterfeit resurrection Revelation 13:3, 12, 14 Yes No Yes · probable Weighed
5 Boasting and blasphemy Revelation 13:1, 5–6 Yes No Yes · probable Weighed
6 Worship demanded Revelation 13:4, 8, 12, 15 Yes Partly Yes · probable Weighed
7 War on the holy ones Revelation 13:7, 15 Yes No Yes · possible Weighed
8 Universal reach Revelation 13:7–8 Yes Yes Yes · possible Weighed
12 A limited time Revelation 13:5 Yes No Yes · possible Weighed
13 A number that calls for wisdom Revelation 13:18 Yes, without a name No Not as a name · grade to come Weighed
14 The response asked of the faithful Revelation 13:9–10 Coming

Marks 4, 9, 10 and 11 belong to the beast from the earth, the false prophet, and will be weighed in the next stage.

Apocalypse 13

Every reading of Revelation 13, Fr. John's included, weighed in the same way: against the marks Scripture and the Church reveal, and against the Church's standards for reading Scripture. Verdicts are on arguments, never on persons.
