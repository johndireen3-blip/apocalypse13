## What the mark requires

Read in context ([Standard 4](/the-standards#s4)), the beast's reach is *authority*, given by the dragon and joined to worship (Revelation 13:4, 8), and in Revelation 17 the kings hand their power to it (17:13). Great powers have reached across the world in every age, and the Church herself calls for a world authority bound by law and the moral order (*Caritas in Veritate* 67), so wide reach is not yet the mark. A reading that claims it must show nations giving up their power to the one it names, or a single authority over them centered on him.

## Verdict on the arguments

Fr. John shows a presidency using its reach over other nations unusually directly, and in his own name: running another country, pressing allies for their land, and chairing a new body of nations. His evidence is documented and his defense is stated. But what it shows is the influence of a great power, contested by most of the world and checked by rivals and allies, not yet the authority over every nation, freely handed over by kings, that the text describes. The Board of Peace is the item that bears most directly on Revelation 17:13 and is worth watching. St. Irenaeus reads the mark faithfully of the one to come. Thiel's world state takes this mark as its center, but his reading identifies a tendency rather than a present power.

These are verdicts on arguments, not on any person.
