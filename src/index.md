## How this site works

Every reading is weighed in two ways. First, against the **marks**: what Scripture and the Church reveal about the beasts, set out in depth on [The First Beast](https://thefirstbeast.com/marks). Second, against the **[standards](/the-standards)**: the Church's own criteria for reading Scripture, and the tests that follow from them.

For each mark, the site asks of every reading the same questions: does it claim the mark, what evidence does it give, what weighs against it, and which standards does it meet. The answer is always a verdict on an **argument**, never on a person. This site never says that anyone is or is not the Antichrist; that is as unknowable as the day and the hour.

## Fr. John's own reading is weighed too

Fr. John, who directs this project, has his own reading of Revelation 13, which he sets out on [his own site](https://apocalypse12.org/). Here it is weighed by exactly the same standards as every other, with the evidence against it set down as faithfully as the evidence for it. That is the point of the site.

## In charity

Revelation 13 is written so that the holy ones may endure in faith (Revelation 13:10), not so that anyone may live in fear or turn against a neighbor. Every reading here is weighed in that spirit, and so is every person discussed.
