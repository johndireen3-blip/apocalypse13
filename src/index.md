## Why this site exists

Since 2024 the technology investor Peter Thiel has lectured widely on the Antichrist, in the United States and abroad, including in Rome, and he has published his reading in *First Things*. His proposal has drawn wide attention, and Catholic voices have answered it. What he argues, and how it fares, is set out on [Peter Thiel and the Antichrist](/peter-thiel-and-the-antichrist). This site sets his proposal beside what Scripture's own notes, the Fathers and the Church's writers have said over the centuries, and weighs each by the same standards. It adds Fr. John's own proposal, weighed in exactly the same way. And it invites others to take part: if you have a reading of Revelation 13 of your own, or thoughts on any of these, write through the [Contact](/contact) page. A proposal sent with its reasons may be stated at its best on [The Proposals](/the-proposals) and weighed like the rest.

## How this site works

Every reading is weighed in two ways. First, against the **marks**: what Scripture and the Church reveal about the beasts, set out in depth on [The First Beast](https://thefirstbeast.com/marks). Second, against the **[standards](/the-standards)**: the Church's own criteria for reading Scripture, and the tests that follow from them.

For each mark, the site asks of every reading the same questions: does it claim the mark, what evidence does it give, what weighs against it, and which standards does it meet. The answer is always a verdict on an **argument**, never on a person. This site never says that anyone is or is not the Antichrist; that is as unknowable as the day and the hour.

## The emblem

The emblem of this site is a plumb line, or plummet: a weight hung on a cord to show whether a wall stands true. The prophet Amos saw the Lord "standing, plummet in hand, by a wall built with a plummet," and heard him say, "See, I am laying the plummet in the midst of my people Israel" (Amos 7:7–8). The plummet does not lean toward any wall; it shows where each one stands. So here every reading of Revelation 13, Fr. John's included, is held to the same line: the marks Scripture reveals and the Church's standards for reading it.

## In charity

Revelation 13 is written so that the holy ones may endure in faith (Revelation 13:10), not so that anyone may live in fear or turn against a neighbor. Every reading here is weighed in that spirit, and so is every person discussed.
