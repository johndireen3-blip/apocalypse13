## What this site is

Many people read Revelation 13 as being fulfilled in our own time, and some name names. This site sets those readings side by side and weighs them in the same way: against the marks Scripture and the Church reveal, set out on [The First Beast](https://thefirstbeast.com/), and against the Church's standards for reading Scripture. Each reading is first stated as its holder would state it. The conclusions are about arguments, never about persons.

## Fr. John

Fr. John is a Catholic diocesan priest. He founded and directs The Ephesus Challenge, a family of websites on the Book of Revelation and Christian hope, and he holds his own reading of Revelation 13, which he sets out on [his own site](https://apocalypse12.org/). On this site his reading is weighed by the same standards as every other, and its weaknesses are stated as plainly as its strengths. This site is researched and written with Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, under his direction. Opinions about any particular reading that are his own are marked as his.

## The emblem

The emblem of this site is a plumb line, or plummet: a weight hung on a cord to show whether a wall stands true. The prophet Amos saw the Lord "standing, plummet in hand, by a wall built with a plummet," and heard him say, "See, I am laying the plummet in the midst of my people Israel" (Amos 7:7–8). The plummet does not lean toward any wall; it shows where each one stands. So here every reading of Revelation 13, Fr. John's included, is held to the same line: the marks Scripture reveals and the Church's standards for reading it.
